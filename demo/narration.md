# Spendlens presentation: narration script

Voiceover for `spendlens-presentation.mp4` (about 3 min 54 s, English, female voice).

## Title

Meet Spendlens, the AI-powered analytics platform for marketing agencies and e-commerce teams. In the next few minutes, I will walk you through everything it offers, step by step.

## The problem

Campaign data is scattered across ad platforms and spreadsheets. Finding the campaigns that lose money takes hours, and nobody is sure the numbers are right. Spendlens brings everything into one clear view, tells you what needs attention, and shows its evidence.

## 1. Create an account and workspace

Step one: create an account and a workspace. Every workspace is completely isolated, so one client can never see another client's data. Passwords are hashed, and login attempts are rate limited.

## 2. Import your data

Step two: bring in your data. Upload a CSV export from your ad platform. If your column names differ, for example in a Meta Ads export, a short mapping screen connects them. Rows with problems are reported with the exact row, column and reason, while valid rows are imported safely. And uploading the same file twice never creates duplicates.

## Switch to the demo workspace

For the rest of the tour, I will switch to a demo workspace with ninety days of campaign data.

## 3. Overview

This is the overview. Eight key metrics, spend, revenue, return on ad spend, conversions, cost per acquisition, click-through rate, cost per click and clicks, each compared with the previous period and shown with a trend line. Changes always appear with an arrow, a sign and a number, never by color alone. Pick seven, thirty or ninety days, or any custom range.

## 4. AI insights

Now, the part that makes Spendlens different. A rules engine detects real problems: a campaign that loses money, a sudden spike in cost per click, conversions falling week after week, a runaway budget, or broken tracking. Then an AI model explains them in plain language, with concrete recommendations. Every number in the answer is checked against your data before you see it. If the check fails, you get the rule-based findings instead, so you are never shown an invented figure.

## 5. Ask your data

You can also simply ask. Type a question in plain English, like which campaign is losing money, or what should I look at first. The model can only read your data through a few safe, read-only tools, and its answer is verified the same way. You can see exactly which tools it used. Off-topic requests, and attempts to override its rules, are politely refused.

## 6. Daily chart

Below, a daily chart of spend and revenue. Switch the metric, hide a series, or flip to a table view for accessibility.

## 7. Campaigns

The campaigns page lists every campaign with all of its metrics. Search, sort by any column, and spot problems at a glance: campaigns returning less than they cost are flagged as losing.

## 8. Compare campaigns

Select up to four campaigns and compare them side by side. The best value in every row is marked, and the chart shows how they evolved. Here you can see the Brand Search conversions collapse on the last day, a tracking problem that the system had already flagged.

## 9. Export, themes and mobile

Export exactly what you see to CSV, protected against spreadsheet formula injection. And Spendlens works in dark or light mode, on desktop and on mobile.

## Built to be trusted

Under the hood, Spendlens is built to be trusted. More than one hundred and seventy automated tests protect every workflow, including data isolation between workspaces. A dedicated evaluation suite measures how accurate the findings are, how often the AI invents a number, its resistance to prompt injection, and the quality of its writing, with quality gates that must pass.

## Closing

Spendlens. From raw campaign data to clear, verified decisions. Thank you for watching.
